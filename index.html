The challenge

Retailers lose high-intent WhatsApp buyers to slow manual replies.

Over 60 million Indian SMBs rely on WhatsApp for customer communication, yet store owners juggle counter billing, inventory, and deliveries simultaneously—leaving customer chats unanswered.

Delayed response = lost conversion

Buyers message multiple local stores at once. The sale goes to whichever store responds first with accurate availability.

Repetitive daily queries

"What's the rate?", "Is this item in stock?", "Do you deliver nearby?" — dozens of repetitive queries consume hours every day.

Missed after-hours demand

Evening and late-night inquiries sit unread until morning, when customer intent has already moved elsewhere.

How the Engine Works

Automated Conversational Commerce Architecture

  • 1. Catalogue Ingestion
    Structured parsing of store SKUs, variants, pricing, and delivery zones into vector memory.
  • 2. Intent & Language Detection
    Real-time classification of product inquiries, stock checks, and purchase intent across Hindi/English.
  • 3. Grounded Response Generation
    Low-latency LLM synthesis strictly constrained to verified store inventory and business rules.
  • 4. Order & Lead Dispatch
    Instant webhook & WhatsApp notification to the merchant once customer details are confirmed.
